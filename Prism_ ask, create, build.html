Questions, answered

Is Prism really free to start?

Yes. Sign in and you get 15 messages, 3 images, 1 motion clip and 1 app or website every day. Limits reset at midnight.

Can it really do anything?

It covers a very wide range: questions, writing, code, images, clips and apps. Video comes as animated HTML clips you can replay and save, not MP4 files. Check important facts before relying on them.

What do Easy, Medium and Hard mean?

They set how deep a chat answer goes. Easy uses plain words and everyday examples, Medium explains the reasoning, and Hard goes to expert detail.

Are my chats private?

Chats and usage are stored on your device in this browser. When Claude is connected, your prompt is sent to Claude so it can write the reply.

Is the payment real?

Not yet. The tpay checkout is a demo that shows how paying would feel. No money moves and nothing is stored.

What if Claude is not connected?

Prism switches to a basic built-in mode. It can still do maths, tell the date and time, explain common topics, and make a simple image, clip or starter page.
